Delivery model: the asset and the pipe

The guide is not a WhatsApp blast or a web page. It is both, each doing the job it is good at. The website is the evergreen home that ranks and converts. WhatsApp is where members live and where live perk codes are shared. The PDF pack is the bridge that travels between them.

The asset

Website

/welcome · source of truth

Evergreen, SEO-ranked, perk-badged, updatable. Ranks for "best cafes Canggu" and pulls in new members. Gated by the join flow so it grows the owned list.

The bridge

PDF Pack

Shareable lead magnet

A branded download that lives on the site and drops straight into WhatsApp as a file. The same artifact works in both channels, online and offline.

The pipe

WhatsApp

Distribution + codes

Where new members land. Pinned welcome links the guide, live discount codes are shared here, and "new spot added" nudges keep it warm.

The perks system

Every venue card has three states. The perk layer is the monetisation and retention engine, and the reason the guide must live on the website where it can be updated the moment a deal is signed.

State A · no deal
Listing only
An honest recommendation. No badge. This is the default and most listings stay here.
State B · community
★ BSTC perk
A signed member benefit: day pass, member rate, free coconut. Badge renders, code shared in WhatsApp.
State C · featured
★ Featured partner
A paid or flagship partner, e.g. TAP. Larger placement, top of category, story in the community.

Perks, rolling out now

We partner with venues across Canggu to give members real perks: day passes, discounts, member rates. When a perk goes live it shows on the listing, and the code is shared inside WhatsApp. TAP Canggu is the first featured partner, free beer on Sundays.

Join to unlock perks › Run a venue? Partner with us

Codes are never stored in the public site data. The badge advertises the perk, WhatsApp delivers the code. That split keeps the value gated to members and gives them a reason to stay in the community.

The brief

Design a single-page promotional flyer for a recurring Sunday padel session at TAP Club, Canggu, co-presented by the Bali Startup & Tech Community. The hero hook is a free beer, an exclusive perk BSTC organised for its community. The mood is vintage sports-club meets editorial print flyer. Tactile, premium, and warm, never a discount blast.

Primary format

A4 portrait

210 × 297mm, print-ready at 300dpi. The master artwork.

Also export

Social crops

4:5 feed (1080 × 1350) and 9:16 story (1080 × 1920) for WhatsApp and Instagram.

BSTC × TAP · Brand CanonicalHard rules
08

Hard rules

Non-negotiable. These keep every asset on-system.

1

Australian English throughout. Organised, colour, centre, optimise.

2

No em or en dashes anywhere. Use commas, full stops, or a middot.

3

Red is reserved. Perk, CTA and stamp only. Never decorative.

4

Hard edges only. No rounded corners, no gradients, no gloss or 3D.

5

One page. Keep the master to a single A4. Crops come after.

6

White space is a feature. Let the blocks breathe, do not fill every gap.

In one line

Vintage sports-club warmth, editorial discipline, and one loud red perk. Play, meet the crew, cold one after.

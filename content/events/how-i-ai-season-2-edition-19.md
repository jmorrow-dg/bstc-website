**How I AI Season 2, Edition 19.** Builders share the AI tools, workflows and revenue behind what they ship. Season 2 runs every Wednesday from 4 November to 9 December 2026, 6:00 to 9:00 PM WITA at Seoul Soul Project, Jl. Canggu Padang Linjong No.61, Canggu.

Locally hosted by Seoul Soul Project · Moderated by Bali Startup & Tech Community

Signal > Noise. No theory. No hype. No "AI will change everything" keynotes.

**The rough schedule:**
- 6:00 PM: Doors open. Informal chat, drinks, settle in
- 6:30 PM: Welcome + The Money Round: share what you've been building or shipping with AI this week, 1 minute each
- 7:00 PM: AI Intel Drop: Josh Morrow breaks down the week in AI
- 7:15 PM: Dinner & drinks break
- 7:30 PM: Builder Spotlights: two builders, 10 minutes each + Q&A. Real tools, real numbers
- 8:00 PM onwards: Dinner, drinks & open networking

**Who this is for:**
- Founders integrating AI into their products
- Engineers building AI-powered features
- Operators automating workflows with AI
- Anyone who wants to stop reading about AI and start profiting from it

Free entry. Order and pay for your own dinner and drinks to support the venue that hosts the community. [RSVP on Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/bali-start-ups-tech-community/events/317128200/), then [join the BSTC community](https://www.balistartupandtech.com/join?src=event-website) for the community chat and a free member profile.

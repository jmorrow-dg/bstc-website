Our 40th Community Networking Dinner/Drinks Event, 6:30 pm on Thursday 10 December 2026 at Yema Kitchen, Canggu. Free to attend. New faces welcome.

**Who's in the room:** real founders, builders and operators in Bali. Indie hackers, agency owners, solo devs, digital nomads and a few investors, all genuinely shipping something. No suits, no hard pitches.

**How the night runs:** relaxed by design. A quick open mic session with a couple of short founder talks (the real story and learnings behind what they're building), then the rest of the night is yours for easy, no-pressure networking.

**The rules:**
- No hard selling
- 60-second intros max
- Signal > Noise

Hosted at Yema Kitchen, Canggu · Moderated by Bali Start-Up & Tech Community.

Free entry. [RSVP on Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/bali-start-ups-tech-community/events/clrtbvyjcqbnb/), then [join the BSTC community](https://www.balistartupandtech.com/join?src=event-website) for the community chat and a free member profile.

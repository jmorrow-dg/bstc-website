BSTC's flagship monthly networking night, on the third Thursday of every month at Yema Kitchen, Canggu.

The room where Bali's best founders, engineers, operators and investors actually meet. No pitching, no panels, no name badges. Just the people building things, in one place.

**Format:**
- 6:30 PM: Arrivals, drinks and open networking
- 7:30 PM: Open mic — 60 seconds each, what you are building and what you need
- 8:00 PM: Dinner and free-flow conversation

**Who comes:**
- Founders from pre-seed through Series A
- Engineers, designers and operators
- Angels and VCs active in Southeast Asia

Free entry. Food and drinks at the venue are on you and support the host. RSVP on MeetUp so we can hold your seat.

The seventeenth edition of **How I AI**: BSTC's builder-focused series about profiting from AI.

Signal > Noise. No theory. No hype. No "AI will change everything" keynotes.

**The theme this week:** capability stopped being the bottleneck. Control is.

**The six signals we covered:**

- **Anthropic ships frontier AI at half the price.** Claude Opus 5 lands close to Fable 5 on coding and knowledge-work benchmarks at roughly half the cost per task, with a low, medium and high effort toggle.
- **An AI broke out of a test and hacked a real company.** Two OpenAI models escaped a sealed cyber evaluation through a zero-day and reached Hugging Face's production systems to steal a benchmark answer key.
- **Thirty rivals team up on AI cyber defence.** Nvidia and 30+ companies launched the Open Secure AI Alliance. OpenAI, Google and Anthropic are not in it.
- **Nvidia may co-sign a $250 billion lease** so OpenAI can take a 10 gigawatt data centre campus in Ohio.
- **The largest open model ever built is now free.** Moonshot published Kimi K3's weights: 2.8 trillion parameters, 1.4 terabytes, free to download.
- **The plug standard for agents got rebuilt.** The 2026-07-28 Model Context Protocol spec dropped sessions and went stateless, the biggest change since launch.

**Plus:** Boris Cherny's 4 Steps of AI Adoption, mapped from Gated through to AI-native, and the five blockers that actually stall teams. The action of the week was to put a fence around one agent: list what it can reach, cut it back to least privilege, cap the spend, and add one review step outside the agent.

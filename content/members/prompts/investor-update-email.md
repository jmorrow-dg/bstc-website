Feed the model your raw numbers and bullet points; it produces the polished update.

## The prompt

> You are helping a startup founder write their monthly investor update. Style: concise, honest, skimmable in 90 seconds. Investors should feel informed and confident, not sold to.
>
> Here are my raw notes for this month: **[paste metrics, wins, lowlights, asks]**.
>
> Structure the update as:
> - **TL;DR** (2 lines)
> - **Metrics** (MRR/ARR, growth %, runway, 1-2 north-star numbers as a simple list)
> - **Wins** (3 bullets)
> - **Lowlights / challenges** (2 bullets, honest)
> - **Asks** (specific intros, hires, or advice, with names where I gave them)
>
> Keep the whole thing under 300 words. Do not invent numbers. Flag anything I left blank.

## Why it works

The "asks" section turns a passive update into dealflow: warm intros, candidate referrals, and follow-on interest. The honesty in lowlights is what builds the trust that closes your next round.

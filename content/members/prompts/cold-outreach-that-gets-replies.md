Use this with Claude or ChatGPT. Paste the prospect's website copy and LinkedIn "About" section where indicated.

## The prompt

> You are a founder writing a short cold email to another founder. Tone: peer-to-peer, specific, zero fluff, no corporate buzzwords.
>
> Here is what my company does: **[1-2 sentences on your product and who it's for]**.
>
> Here is the prospect: **[paste their website copy + LinkedIn About]**.
>
> Write a 60-90 word cold email that:
> 1. Opens with one specific, genuine observation about THEIR business (not flattery).
> 2. Connects that observation to a concrete problem we solve, in one line.
> 3. Makes one small, low-friction ask (a 15-min call or a yes/no question).
> 4. Has no links, no attachments, no "hope this finds you well."
>
> Give me 3 subject-line options under 5 words each. Then the email.

## Why it works

The specificity in step 1 is what separates a reply from a delete. Make the model do the research synthesis so every email feels hand-written, even at volume.

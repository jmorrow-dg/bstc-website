Use this to generate an interview guide before customer calls.

## The prompt

> You are an expert in customer discovery and the principles of "The Mom Test". I'm investigating this problem space: **[describe the problem and who has it]**.
>
> Generate a 20-minute interview guide that:
> 1. Asks about the person's actual past behaviour and specific recent examples, never hypotheticals ("would you...").
> 2. Digs into how they currently solve this, what it costs them (time / money / pain), and what they've already tried.
> 3. Avoids pitching my idea or leading the witness.
> 4. Ends with questions that surface who else has this problem and how acute it is.
>
> Give me 8-10 questions ordered from broad to specific, plus 3 follow-up probes I can use when an answer is vague.

## Why it works

The model defaults to leading, validation-seeking questions. Explicitly anchoring it to The Mom Test forces behaviour-based questions that give you the truth, not encouragement.

# Running an agency from Bali: visas, clients, hiring and time zones

You can run an agency from Bali if your clients and your company sit outside Indonesia: that's what the E33G remote worker visa is built around, and Bali's UTC+8 clock suits Australian, Singapore and Asian clients well. Taking Indonesian clients, or employing Indonesians directly, is a separate setup that needs a local company and the right work permits. The hard parts are US and UK time zones, hiring properly, and keeping your connection up.

It's written for dev, design, marketing and AI automation agency owners. It isn't legal, tax or immigration advice: read our [visa guide](/blog/indonesia-startup-visa-guide) and [tax guide](/blog/bali-founder-tax-guide-2026), then ask a lawyer about your case.

## What's legal on which visa

Work for clients outside Indonesia through a company outside Indonesia, and don't sell to anyone in Indonesia until you've set up locally. From imigrasi's own pages, checked 26 Oct 2026:

| Visa | What imigrasi says it's for | What it rules out | Fees listed |
|---|---|---|---|
| [E33G remote worker](https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/E33G) | Staying in Indonesia "to carry out tasks from a foreign company". Needs an employment agreement with a company established outside Indonesia, income of at least US$60,000 a year and US$2,000 in the bank. Stay of up to a year, extendable online | Working in a way that doesn't match the permit, and selling goods or services "unless needed in their work" | Rp7,000,000 for a one-year stay |
| [C2 business visit](https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/C2) | Business, meetings, buying goods, negotiating and signing agreements. 60 days, extendable to 180 days in total | Selling goods or services, or taking wages or payment from people or companies in Indonesia | Rp2,000,000 for 60 days |
| [E23 work visa](https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/E23) | Working with a sponsor in Indonesia. Needs a work permit from the Ministry of Manpower. 180 days, one year or two years | Needs a sponsor, so you can't apply alone | See imigrasi's page |

**Where the sources don't line up.** Our [visa guide](/blog/indonesia-startup-visa-guide) describes the E33G as being for people "employed by, or running" a company outside Indonesia. Imigrasi's page only mentions an employment agreement with a foreign company, and doesn't say whether an owner contracting with their own overseas agency qualifies. We couldn't confirm this on 26 Oct 2026, so ask an immigration lawyer before you apply. Our visa guide also refers to the business visit visa as the B211A; imigrasi's own list now calls it the C2.

**Indonesian clients.** If a Bali villa company or a Jakarta brand wants to hire your agency, you're selling services in Indonesia, which the C2 rules out in plain words. The usual route is a PT PMA, a foreign-owned Indonesian company, which can sponsor your work visa. Our [PT PMA playbook](/blog/pt-pma-indonesia-founder-playbook-2026) walks through the setup.

One update to that playbook: under BKPM Regulation 5 of 2025, a PT PMA needs at least IDR 2.5 billion of paid-up capital per business activity, which stays in the company account for 12 months unless it's spent on capital items or running costs, inside a total investment plan of IDR 10 billion ([Withers, 10 Oct 2025](https://www.withersworldwide.com/en-gb/insight/read/indonesia-eases-foreign-capital-rules-key-takeaways-from-bkpm-regulation-no-5-of-2025), checked 26 Oct 2026). That's real money, so wait until local work justifies it.

## Where the agency entity sits

If your clients are outside Indonesia, the simple setup is a company outside Indonesia too: in your home country, Singapore, the US or another base that fits your clients and your tax position. Clients sign with that company and pay it, and it fits what the E33G expects. Our [visa guide](/blog/indonesia-startup-visa-guide) describes the same pattern, with a PT PMA only once there's a local reason for one.

## Time zones: what a Bali workday overlaps

Bali runs on WITA (Waktu Indonesia Tengah), which a 1987 presidential decree set at GMT+8 for Bali ([Keppres 41/1987](https://www.peraturan.go.id/files/kp41-1987.pdf), checked 26 Oct 2026). Indonesia doesn't use daylight saving, so your clients' clocks move and yours doesn't.

The table shows each city's 9am to 5pm in Bali time. Clock change dates are from the [NSW Government](https://www.nsw.gov.au/about-nsw/daylight-saving), [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/when-do-the-clocks-change) and [NIST](https://www.nist.gov/pml/time-and-frequency-division/popular-links/daylight-saving-time-dst) (US rule), checked 26 Oct 2026; Melbourne changes on Sydney's dates, and we worked the offsets out with the IANA time zone database.

| City | Season (2026 to 2027) | Hours vs Bali | Their 9am to 5pm in Bali time | Overlap with a Bali 9 to 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sydney and Melbourne | Daylight saving, 4 Oct 2026 to 4 Apr 2027 | 3 ahead | 6am to 2pm | 5 hours |
| Sydney and Melbourne | Standard time, 4 Apr to 3 Oct 2027 | 2 ahead | 7am to 3pm | 6 hours |
| Singapore | No daylight saving | Same | 9am to 5pm | 8 hours |
| London | GMT, 25 Oct 2026 to 28 Mar 2027 | 8 behind | 5pm to 1am | None |
| London | BST, 28 Mar to 31 Oct 2027 | 7 behind | 4pm to midnight | 1 hour |
| New York | EST, 1 Nov 2026 to 14 Mar 2027 | 13 behind | 10pm to 6am | None |
| New York | EDT, 14 Mar to 7 Nov 2027 | 12 behind | 9pm to 5am | None |
| San Francisco | PST, 1 Nov 2026 to 14 Mar 2027 | 16 behind | 1am to 9am | None |
| San Francisco | PDT, 14 Mar to 7 Nov 2027 | 15 behind | Midnight to 8am | None |

What that means in practice:

- **Singapore and Australian clients** fit a normal day (if you're weighing a Singapore base instead, see [Bali vs Singapore](/bali-vs/singapore)). In Australian summer, protect your mornings, because Sydney's working day ends at 2pm your time.
- **UK and European clients** get your late afternoon. A 4pm to 7pm Bali window is 8 to 11am in London in winter and 9am to noon in summer.
- **US East Coast clients** mean a 7 to 9am Bali slot (the previous evening in New York) or a 9 to 11pm Bali slot (their morning).
- **US West Coast clients** get your early morning: 7 to 9am Bali is 3 to 5pm the previous day in San Francisco in winter, and 4 to 6pm in summer.

Around the March, April, October and November clock changes, check a converter before you send invites. For running a team across these gaps, see [running a remote team across time zones](https://remotebuilders.io/guides/run-a-remote-team-across-time-zones/?src=bstc-running-an-agency-from-bali).

## Hiring in Indonesia

Our [hiring developers guide](/blog/hiring-developers-indonesia-founders-guide-2026) covers sourcing and vetting. The legal side comes down to three routes.

| Route | How it works | What to watch |
|---|---|---|
| Contractor | They invoice your overseas company | Our hiring guide warns that someone working full time, only for you, on your schedule can be treated as an employee under Indonesian law |
| Employer of record (EOR) | A provider employs them in Indonesia and you pay a monthly fee | [Deel](https://www.deel.com/pricing/) and [Remote](https://remote.com/pricing) both listed EOR from US$599 per employee per month (checked 26 Oct 2026) |
| Your own PT PMA | Your Indonesian company employs them directly | Paid-up capital, local payroll and social security registration, and an annual compliance load (see the [PT PMA playbook](/blog/pt-pma-indonesia-founder-playbook-2026)) |

For a pay floor in Bali: the 2026 provincial minimum wage is Rp3,207,459 a month, and Badung, the regency that includes Canggu, set Rp3,791,002.57 ([Kompas TV, 26 Dec 2025](https://www.kompas.tv/regional/639859/upah-minimum-bali-2026-naik-umk-badung-paling-tinggi), checked 26 Oct 2026). Treat it as a legal floor, not a market rate. We haven't quoted market salaries here because we couldn't find a dated, published source for agency roles in Bali.

For hiring people outside Indonesia, see [hiring remote employees internationally](https://remotebuilders.io/guides/hire-remote-employees-internationally/?src=bstc-running-an-agency-from-bali).

## Internet reliability and backups

An agency lives on calls and deadlines, so plan for the connection to fail.

- **Two connections, two networks.** A fixed line at home plus mobile data on a different provider, so one fault can't take out both.
- **A coworking fallback.** Know which space you'll go to when the villa line drops, and keep a day pass in your budget.
- **Don't count on Starlink.** Starlink stopped taking new customers across Indonesia from 13 July 2025, saying capacity had sold out ([Investortrust, 15 Jul 2025](https://investortrust.id/business/72647/starlink-hentikan-layanan-pelanggan-baru-di-indonesia-ini-kata-operator-lokal), checked 26 Oct 2026). We couldn't confirm on 26 Oct 2026 whether new orders have reopened.
- **Plan around Nyepi.** In 2026, mobile data and local broadcasts in Bali were scheduled to be switched off for 24 hours from 6am on 19 March, with fibre wifi left on ([What's New Indonesia, 13 Mar 2026](https://whatsnewindonesia.com/bali/feature/will-internet-still-be-accessible-bali-during-nyepi-2026), checked 26 Oct 2026). Nyepi 2027 is on Monday 8 March, and Idul Fitri follows on 10 and 11 March ([Setneg](https://setneg.go.id/baca/index/inilah_skb_3_menteri_libur_nasional_dan_cuti_bersama_2027), checked 26 Oct 2026). We couldn't find a 2027 notice yet. Warn clients and don't schedule launches that week.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I run my agency from Bali on the E33G remote worker visa?

The E33G is for working in Indonesia for a company established outside Indonesia, with an employment agreement and income of at least US$60,000 a year. Imigrasi's page doesn't say how that applies to an owner contracting with their own overseas agency, so confirm your case with an immigration lawyer.

### Can I take Indonesian clients while living in Bali?

Not on a visit or remote worker visa. The C2 business visit visa bans selling services or taking payment from people or companies in Indonesia, and the E33G is tied to work for a foreign company. Indonesian clients usually mean a PT PMA, an Indonesian company, plus a work visa and work permit sponsored through it.

### What time zone is Bali in, and which clients does it suit?

Bali uses WITA, UTC+8, all year. It suits Singapore and Australian clients best: Sydney and Melbourne are two or three hours ahead depending on their daylight saving. London gets a late-afternoon window, and US clients mean early mornings or late evenings.

### How do I hire Indonesian staff for my agency?

There are three routes: contractors, an employer of record that employs them for you, or your own PT PMA. Deel and Remote both listed EOR from US$599 per employee per month on 26 October 2026. A PT PMA costs more to set up but puts staff on your own payroll.

### Is the internet in Bali good enough for client calls?

It can be, but don't rely on one connection. Keep a fixed line plus mobile data on a different network, and a coworking space as a fallback. Starlink stopped taking new Indonesian customers in July 2025, and mobile data was switched off over Nyepi in 2026.

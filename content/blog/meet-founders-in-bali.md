# How to meet founders and tech people in Bali

The fastest way to meet founders in Bali is to go to the same weekly event two or three times in a row, then follow up in the community chat. In Canggu in November and December 2026 that's BSTC's free How I AI night every Wednesday at Seoul Soul Project, plus the monthly networking drinks and dinner (next one Thursday 12 November at Yema Kitchen). Add a coworking space where builders work and you'll know a useful group of people within two weeks.

Most people do it the other way round: they join five WhatsApp groups, lurk, go to one event, and leave Bali knowing nobody. Repetition beats volume. People here remember the face they've seen three Wednesdays running, not the one who turned up once.

The format is a one-minute "Money Round" where people share what they've built or shipped with AI that week, a short AI news rundown from Josh Morrow, then two builder spotlights of ten minutes each with Q&A, and open networking over dinner ([event page](/events/how-i-ai-season-2-edition-18); [Meetup listing](https://www.meetup.com/bali-start-ups-tech-community/), checked 25 Oct 2026). Season 1 ran 17 editions at the same venue. Season 2 runs six Wednesdays.

**The networking drinks and dinner** is the broader room: founders, indie hackers, agency owners, solo devs and a few investors. A short open mic, a couple of founder talks, then networking. #39 is on Thursday 12 November 2026 at 6:30pm ([event page](/events/networking-drinks-dinner-canggu-39)).

## Your first week, day by day

This assumes you land early in the week and base yourself in or near Canggu.

1. **Before you land:** sign up at [/join](/join?src=blog-meet-founders-in-bali-inline) so you're in the Telegram chat from day one, and RSVP for the next How I AI on the [events page](/events). Write one line on what you're building and one line on what you need. You'll use it all week.
2. **Day 1 to 2:** pick a coworking space (see below) and buy a few day passes rather than a month. Say hello in the community chat with your two lines.
3. **Wednesday:** go to How I AI. Arrive at 6pm when doors open. It's the easiest hour to talk to people, before the programme starts. In the Money Round, say what you've built this week in one minute.
4. **Thursday or Friday:** message the two or three people you had a real conversation with. Suggest a coffee or a coworking day, not a call.
5. **Weekend:** if you're a developer, check GDG Bali for a weekend meetup in Denpasar.
6. **Second Wednesday:** go back. This is the one most people skip, and it's the one that counts.
7. **The monthly dinner:** go to the next one (12 November in 2026). By now you'll recognise faces, which makes a bigger room much easier.

## Etiquette: how to be someone people want to see again

BSTC's event rules are short: no hard selling, 60-second intros max, signal over noise ([event page](/events/networking-drinks-dinner-canggu-39)). In practice:

- **Don't pitch on the first conversation.** Ask what they're building and what's hard about it. If what you sell is relevant, they'll ask.
- **Be specific.** Something like "I'm building a scheduling tool for physios, 40 paying clinics, stuck on hiring a second dev" gets you an intro. "I'm in tech" gets you a nod.
- **Order something.** The venues host for free and ask you to buy dinner or drinks. It's how the events stay free.
- **Don't add everyone on LinkedIn at the table.** Follow up the next day with one line that references what you talked about.
- **Give before you ask.** Answer someone's question in the chat before you post your own request.
- **Respect the island.** These events depend on local venues that host for free. Treat staff and neighbours well.

More detail in [how to network at tech events](/blog/how-to-network-at-tech-events), and if you're looking for a partner specifically, [how to find a technical co-founder in Bali](/blog/find-technical-cofounder-bali).

## What doesn't work

- **Only joining chats.** Group chats help you find events. They don't replace showing up.
- **One-off visits.** One event gets you business cards. Three gets you people who'll reply.
- **Assuming a listing is current.** Plenty of Bali event pages are years old. Check for a date in the current month before you go. Our [communities directory](/blog/tech-communities-in-bali) shows the last dated event for each group.

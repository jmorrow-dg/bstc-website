# New to Bali? The first-week checklist for remote entrepreneurs (2026)

Your first week in Bali comes down to five jobs: arrive on the right visa, get through the airport with the arrival card and tourist levy done, get a working phone and a legal way to get around, set up money that doesn't bleed fees, and get into a room with people building what you're building. This moving to Bali checklist puts those jobs in order, with every rule and fee linked to the page we checked it on (26 October 2026). It's for SaaS founders, agency owners, ecom operators, freelancers turning into businesses and creators with products, not just startup founders.

Rules here change often, sometimes with little notice. This guide isn't legal, tax or immigration advice; for that, start with our [visa guide](/blog/indonesia-startup-visa-guide) and [tax guide](/blog/bali-founder-tax-guide-2026), then talk to a licensed agent or adviser.

## Before you fly

### Passport

Your passport needs to be valid for at least six months after you arrive and have at least two blank pages, according to the [UK government's Indonesia entry advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/indonesia/entry-requirements) (updated 18 Sep 2026, checked 26 Oct 2026). Indonesia's own visa pages also ask for six months' validity.

### Pick your visa

This is the decision that matters most, because the wrong visa limits what you can legally do once you're here.

| Visa | Stay | Official fee | Good for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Visa on arrival (B1), or e-VOA if applied for online | 30 days, extendable once to 60 | IDR 500,000 | Short first trips | Needs a return or onward ticket; fee rise announced |
| C1 visit visa | 60 days, extendable to 180 | IDR 1,000,000 | A longer first stay | Apply online before you fly; US$2,000 bank statement |
| E33G remote worker visa | 1 year | IDR 7,000,000 | Working for an overseas company | US$60,000 a year income and a contract with a foreign company |
| Second home, Golden Visa, KITAS via your own PT PMA | Years | Varies | Long-term bases | See our visa and PT PMA guides |

Sources: Indonesia's immigration pages for the [B1 visa on arrival](https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/B1), the [C1 visit visa](https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/C1) and the [E33G remote worker visa](https://www.imigrasi.go.id/wna/daftar-visa-indonesia/E33G), plus the [E33G page on the official eVisa site](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/front/faq/e076131c-0d39-469b-afaf-75fc66aff923), all checked 26 Oct 2026.

A few things those pages say that most guides skip:

- **The visa on arrival price may be changing.** The official B1 page still showed IDR 500,000 on 26 Oct 2026. But on 12 Aug 2026, Indonesia's state broadcaster [RRI reported](https://rri.co.id/en/national/2646854/indonesia-implements-tiered-visa-fees-for-efficient-arrivals) a tiered fee of IDR 750,000 for an e-VOA bought before departure and IDR 1 million on arrival, with no start date given. We couldn't find an official start date on 26 Oct 2026, so check [evisa.imigrasi.go.id](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id) when you book. The e-VOA is probably the cheaper and quicker option either way.
- **Visit visas don't cover local work.** The C1 page says holders can't sell goods or services or take pay from people or companies in Indonesia. It also says C1 holders can't appear as a speaker at an event. Neither the B1 nor the C1 page lists remote work as a permitted activity.
- **The E33G is the one built for remote work.** The eVisa site says it lets you "carry out assignments from overseas company" for up to a year, with re-entry allowed. It asks for a bank account showing at least US$60,000 a year in salary or income, an employment contract with a company set up outside Indonesia, a three-month bank statement with at least US$2,000, a CV and an itinerary. You must enter within 90 days of the visa being issued. If you're self-employed or paid by your own overseas company, ask an agent how you'd show the contract; we couldn't find official guidance for that case on 26 Oct 2026.

Our [Indonesia visa guide](/blog/indonesia-startup-visa-guide) covers the longer-term routes, and the Remote Builders [digital nomad visa comparison](https://remotebuilders.io/guides/digital-nomad-visas-for-founders/?src=bstc-new-to-bali-first-week-checklist) shows how Indonesia stacks up against other countries.

### Fill in the All Indonesia arrival card

Since 1 October 2025, every international arrival has had to complete one declaration covering immigration, customs, health and quarantine through **All Indonesia**, on [allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id](https://allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id) or its app ([immigration press release](https://www.imigrasi.go.id/siaran_pers/mulai-1-oktober-deklarasi-kedatangan-penumpang-wajib-dilakukan-di-aplikasi-all-indonesia), checked 26 Oct 2026). You can fill it in from three days before arrival. It's free: the UK advice warns against lookalike sites that charge for it. Skip it and you'll be checked manually at the counter, which the immigration minister said takes longer.

### Pay the Bali tourist levy

Bali charges foreign tourists **IDR 150,000 per person, once per trip**, under the Love Bali scheme ([official Love Bali FAQ](https://lovebali.baliprov.go.id/faq), checked 26 Oct 2026). Pay online before you fly, at the payment counters at Bali's airport and port, or through registered hotels and travel agents. Cards, bank transfer, virtual account, UnionPay and QRIS are accepted, and the QR voucher arrives by email.

KITAS and KITAP holders are exempt by showing their card. Golden Visa holders and holders of some other visas have to apply for the exemption through the Love Bali site.

### Customs basics

From [Indonesian customs](https://www.beacukai.go.id/barang-penumpang) (checked 26 Oct 2026):

- Personal goods worth up to **US$500 per person per arrival** come in free of duty and tax; you pay on the excess.
- Duty-free limits for adults: **1 litre of alcohol** (21 and over) and **200 cigarettes**, 25 cigars or 100g of tobacco. Extra is destroyed.
- Declare **cash of IDR 100 million or more** (or the same in foreign currency).

Your customs declaration is now part of the All Indonesia form.

## At the airport

1. Have your All Indonesia QR code, your e-VOA or visa, and proof of an onward ticket ready.
2. If you didn't get an e-VOA, the visa on arrival is paid at the designated bank counter in the immigration area (per the B1 page).
3. Have your Love Bali voucher on your phone, or pay at the counter.
4. If you'll use an Indonesian SIM for more than 90 days, register your phone's IMEI at customs before you leave the arrivals area (see the next section).

## Days 1 to 3

### Phone, SIM and the IMEI rule

You have three options:

- **Roam on your home SIM.** No registration needed ([Bea Cukai Ngurah Rai](https://ngurahrai.beacukai.go.id/mandatory/registrasi-imei.html), published 8 Aug 2023, checked 26 Oct 2026). Expensive for data, but useful for keeping two-factor codes coming in. The Remote Builders guide to [keeping your phone number abroad](https://remotebuilders.io/guides/keep-your-phone-number-abroad/?src=bstc-new-to-bali-first-week-checklist) covers cheaper ways to do that.
- **A tourist SIM or eSIM.** Telkomsel says its Tourist Prepaid Card can be ordered on its website and collected when you land ([Telkomsel FAQ](https://www.telkomsel.com/support/faq/apa-itu-tourist-prepaid-card)). An undated [Telkomsel article](https://www.telkomsel.com/jelajah/jelajah-lifestyle/esim-telkomsel-paket-dan-harganya) lists "WNA Tourist Starterpack" eSIM packs from IDR 150,000 for 25GB to IDR 800,000 for 200GB, each for 30 days (both checked 26 Oct 2026). Telkomsel's eSIM product page blocked our request, so confirm prices in store.
- **A regular local SIM** once you're settled.

**The IMEI rule:** a phone bought overseas needs its IMEI registered before an Indonesian SIM will work in it. Customs at Bali's airport says:

- Staying **90 days or less**: register at the mobile operator's outlet when you buy the SIM.
- Staying **longer than 90 days**: register through the Bea Cukai website or the Mobile Beacukai app, up to two devices per passenger.
- Register **inside the arrivals customs area** and the US$500 duty and tax exemption applies. Register after you've left, within 60 days of arrival, and there's no exemption, so you may pay import duty and tax on the phone.

### Getting around

Most newcomers use the Grab and [Gojek](https://www.gojek.com/en-id/) apps for rides and food. The UK government suggests checking that the driver matches the one in the app, and sharing your trip with a friend ([UK safety advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/indonesia/safety-and-security), checked 26 Oct 2026). Grab's website was down for maintenance when we checked.

**Scooters need the right licence.** Here's what we could confirm on 26 Oct 2026:

- Bali's Governor's Circular 7 of 2025 says foreign tourists must hold a valid international or national driving licence, wear a helmet and not ride under the influence ([Bali government](https://bmc.baliprov.go.id/news/title/tertibkan-turis-asing-yang-berulah-di-bali-gubernur-koster-keluarkan-se-nomor-7-tahun-2025), 24 Mar 2025).
- Under Indonesia's road traffic law, riding without a licence carries up to four months' jail or a fine of up to IDR 1 million (Article 281); riding without a helmet, up to IDR 250,000 (Article 291) ([Indonesian National Police summary](https://pusiknas.polri.go.id/web_pusiknas/PPP/Ketentuan%20dan%20Denda%20Resmi%20Pelnggaran%20Lalu%20Lintas.pdf)).
- An International Driving Permit (IDP) is a 1949-convention document you get at home. In Australia, only state and territory motoring clubs issue it; online "international driving licences" aren't IDPs ([NRMA](https://www.mynrma.com.au/travel/international-driving-permits)).
- NRMA says Australians need an IDP **and** a valid Australian motorcycle licence before riding a moped overseas, and that without them your travel insurance could be void ([NRMA, 15 Oct 2025](https://www.mynrma.com.au/open-road/advice-and-how-to/travel-advice/warning-about-overseas-scooter-accidents)). If you're from somewhere else, check the same two things with whoever issues IDPs in your country.
- Once you hold a KITAS, you can apply for an Indonesian licence, according to the [UK government](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/indonesia/safety-and-security).

If you don't hold a motorcycle licence, use the apps or hire a driver. The UK government's advice notes motorbike accidents in Bali, some fatal.

### Money

The short version: bring a multi-currency card and a backup card, take rupiah from bank ATMs, always choose to be charged in rupiah, and only change cash at licensed money changers. Most people on visit visas can't open an Indonesian bank account, because the big banks ask for a KITAS or KITAP. QRIS works for visitors whose home banking app is linked to it, which covers six countries so far. Full details, fees and sources: **[money and banking in Bali for foreigners](/blog/banking-in-bali-for-foreigners)**.

### Make sure your stay is registered

If you stay in private accommodation, the UK government says you must register with the local police and could be fined IDR 5 million if you don't; hotels do it for you ([UK entry advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/indonesia/entry-requirements), checked 26 Oct 2026). If you've booked a villa, ask the host whether they've registered you.

## Weeks two to four

- **Diary your visa deadline.** Extend a visa on arrival within its first 30 days; overstaying costs IDR 1 million a day, according to the [UK government](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/indonesia/entry-requirements). e-VOA extensions are done online.
- **Count your days.** How long you spend in Indonesia can make you an Indonesian tax resident, whatever your visa says. Read our [Bali tax guide](/blog/bali-founder-tax-guide-2026) and the Remote Builders guide to [tax residency for remote founders](https://remotebuilders.io/guides/tax-residency-for-remote-founders/?src=bstc-new-to-bali-first-week-checklist).
- **Decide whether you need an Indonesian company.** Most people selling overseas don't. If you want to hire locally, invoice Indonesian clients or sponsor your own KITAS, read the [PT PMA playbook](/blog/pt-pma-indonesia-founder-playbook-2026). If you're still choosing where to incorporate, see Remote Builders on [where to incorporate as a remote founder](https://remotebuilders.io/guides/where-to-incorporate-remote-founder/?src=bstc-new-to-bali-first-week-checklist).
- **Get paid properly.** Business accounts, Stripe and merchants of record are covered in the [banking guide](/blog/banking-in-bali-for-foreigners).
- **Go back to the same event.** The second and third Wednesday are when the useful conversations start.

## Frequently asked questions

### What do I need to do before flying to Bali?

Check your passport has at least six months left and blank pages, choose and apply for your visa (an e-VOA, a C1 visit visa or the E33G remote worker visa), fill in the free All Indonesia arrival card in the three days before you land, and pay the IDR 150,000 Bali tourist levy on the Love Bali website. Sign up at /join too, so you're in the community chat when you land.

### How much is the Bali tourist levy in 2026?

IDR 150,000 per foreign tourist, paid once per trip, according to the official Love Bali FAQ checked on 26 October 2026. Pay it on lovebali.baliprov.go.id before you fly, or at the counters at Bali's airport. KITAS and KITAP holders are exempt by showing their card.

### Can I work remotely in Bali on a visa on arrival?

The visa on arrival and C1 visit visa pages don't list remote work as a permitted activity, and both bar you from selling goods or services or taking pay from anyone in Indonesia. The E33G remote worker visa is the one designed for working for an overseas company. Get advice from a visa agent or lawyer for your situation.

### Do I need to register my phone's IMEI in Indonesia?

Only if you put an Indonesian SIM in a phone bought overseas. Roaming on your home SIM doesn't need it. If you're staying 90 days or less, the operator's outlet registers it; if you're staying longer, register with customs, ideally in the arrivals hall where the US$500 exemption applies.

### Can I ride a scooter in Bali with my car licence?

Don't count on it. Bali's 2025 rules for foreign tourists require a valid international or national driving licence, and Australia's NRMA warns that without an International Driving Permit and a valid motorcycle licence from home, your travel insurance could be void.
